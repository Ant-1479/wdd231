About Akos Luxe

Accessories that make an outfit feel finished.

What we sell

Akos Luxe is a fashion accessories business for women and young adults. We sell ladies' bags, shoes and slippers in black, gold, pink and ivory, so there is something for work, church, parties and relaxing at home.

Why this website exists

Customers used to message us to ask what was in stock, what things cost and what a bag or pair of shoes looked like. This site puts all of that in one place. You can search, compare, check prices in cedis and see the dollar equivalent, then send your order when you are ready.

Our promise

  • Fashionable pieces at fair prices.
  • Honest product descriptions and clear availability.
  • A real person replies to every order and enquiry.
