Site Name

Discover Ghana

The name "Discover Ghana" was selected because the website focuses on promoting Ghana's culture, tourist attractions, history, food, and travel experiences.

Site Purpose

The website serves as a guide for tourists and residents interested in exploring Ghana. It provides information on attractions, cultural events, local foods, travel tips, and recommended destinations.

Scenarios

  • What are the best tourist attractions to visit in Ghana?
  • Where can I find information about cultural festivals in Ghana?
  • What local foods should visitors try?
  • What travel tips should first-time visitors know?
