Born from
obsession.

We didn't set out to build a fragrance brand. We set out to find the most extraordinary raw materials in the world and ask what happened when you put them together.

The Beginning

A perfumer, a market,
and a piece of wild oud.

It began in 2016, in a spice market in Accra. Our founder, Ama Kojo, bought a small piece of agarwood from a trader who couldn't name the price — only that it had come a very long way.

Two years of sourcing, study, and obsessive blending later, Akos Fragrance was born — its name from the Akan word for "warrior woman," a fragrance house with something to prove.

We believe that fragrance is the most intimate art form. Unlike a painting or a sculpture, it lives on your skin, evolves with your body chemistry, and disappears — leaving only a memory.

The Craft

Two years to perfect
a single formula.

We do not have a production timeline. A fragrance is finished when it is finished. Midnight Oud took 26 months and 340 trials before the formula was locked. We consider this normal.

Our head perfumer works with a library of over 800 raw materials sourced from 42 countries. Every ingredient is evaluated in isolation, then in combination, until the composition reveals its character.

We use no synthetic shortcuts where a natural exists. We use no filler materials to extend volume. What is in the bottle is only what the fragrance demands.

What We Stand For

Three principles.
No exceptions.

01

Rarity over volume

We produce a maximum of 500 bottles per fragrance per year. Scarcity is not a strategy — it is a consequence of sourcing the best ingredients in the world.

02

Patience over speed

No fragrance leaves our atelier until the formula is perfect. We have cancelled completed batches. We have restarted. We consider this the minimum standard.

03

Transparency over mystique

We list every ingredient. We name every source. We believe you deserve to know exactly what you are wearing and where it came from.

How We Work

The Akos Process

Step 01

Sourcing

We travel to origin. Every material is evaluated at source — from oud forests in Southeast Asia to rose fields in Bulgaria. Traceability is non-negotiable.

Step 02

Composition

Our perfumer works in private, without brief or deadline, until the composition reveals itself. This takes months. Sometimes years. We do not rush.

Step 03

Evaluation

Each composition is evaluated on skin across three seasons before approval. Fragrances that fail in summer or winter are revised. There are no shortcuts.

Step 04

Bottling

Fragrances are bottled by hand in our Accra atelier, labelled with the batch number and date, and quality-checked individually before release.
